# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers, who
will review and take appropriate action, including warnings or bans from the
project. This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces.
